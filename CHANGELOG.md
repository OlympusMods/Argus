# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Hold or toggle zoom with scroll steps, a finer step on a second key, a maximum up to 1000x and an unlimited mode
- Starting zoom level, remember the last level across restarts, smooth transitions and a reset key or middle click
- Mouse sensitivity that follows the zoom, cinematic smoothing, and aim that stays smooth at very high zoom
- Hide the crosshair, hand or HUD while zoomed, a vignette and a zoom level indicator
- Scroll out past 1x to pull the camera back in third person or while freelooking, or to widen the FOV in first person
- Freelook in first and third person with hold or toggle, smooth, instant or no return, its own sensitivity, pitch limits, invert pitch and a head that follows the camera
- Settings in a card of the Proteus menu and in config/argus.json
